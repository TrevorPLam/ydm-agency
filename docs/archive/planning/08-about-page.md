**H1:** About YDM Agency
**Subhead:** A modern marketing firm built for small businesses that want high‑quality work, direct communication, and results — without the overhead of a traditional agency.

**How YDM Agency Works** — Created to give businesses a smarter alternative: a focused, lean operation using modern AI‑accelerated tools to deliver custom websites, search visibility, and marketing systems, faster and more affordably than a legacy agency. Three principles: **Direct collaboration** (talk to the professional doing the work) · **Modern execution** (latest stacks handle repetitive research/accelerate dev, strategy/creativity/QC stay human) · **Radical transparency** (real‑time dashboards, plain‑English reports, no long‑term lock‑in). Deliberately lean — not from limitation, but because it keeps communication clear, overhead low, quality high.

**What YDM Agency Believes:** Results over reports (goal is leads/sales/visibility, not jargon PDFs) · no templates/shortcuts (custom‑built with Next.js/Tailwind, designed to grow with the business) · accessibility & performance by default (WCAG 2.1 AA, optimized day one) · no lock‑in (clients stay because the work performs).

**What Sets It Apart:** AI‑augmented, human‑directed (ships in weeks not months) · direct access to the person doing the work · documented transparent process (every service has a process page → `/services/process`) · fair straightforward pricing (no management layers).

**Proof of Capability** — new firm, but quality immediately visible via: detailed service‑specific process pages (see delivery before contact) · commitment to transparency (dashboards, monthly reporting, open comms) · clear service and deliverable definitions. As real client projects complete, case studies/testimonials will be added; until then, process and service detail speak for themselves.

**Where Based:** Fully remote operation — keeps costs low, serves clients efficiently regardless of time zone.
