## Process Hub (`/services/process`)
**H1:** How Projects Come Together at YDM Agency
**Subhead:** A structured, transparent approach that turns your goals into a finished project — on time, without surprises.

**Client Lifecycle**
1. **Initial Conversation** — short call/questionnaire captures business, challenges, success definition; no pitch/pressure. *Receive: discussion summary + next‑steps decision.*
2. **Scope & Proposal** — deliverables/timeline/investment defined concretely, every assumption documented. *Receive: written proposal, fixed‑price scope or transparent estimate.*
3. **Delivery & Collaboration** — phased work, regular previews/feedback loops, AI‑augmented tools accelerate without cutting corners, no black boxes. *Receive: staging links/dashboards/draft deliverables depending on service.*
4. **Launch & Handoff** — performance/accessibility/integration testing before deployment; final files, docs, training handed over. *Receive: live functioning deliverable + continued availability for questions.*
5. **Ongoing Support (Optional)** — maintenance/SEO/content retainers available; relationship doesn't end at launch unless preferred. *Receive: optional ongoing plans.*

**Service‑Specific Processes:** Web Design → `/services/web-design/process` · SEO & AI Search → `/services/seo/process` · Maintenance → `/services/maintenance/process` · Analytics → `/services/analytics/process` · Paid Advertising → `/services/paid-ads/process` `[SELECT-CLIENTS]` · Branding → `/services/branding/process` · Content & Copywriting → `/services/content/process` · CRM & Automation → `/services/automation/process` `[SELECT-CLIENTS]` · Reputation → `/services/reputation/process` `[SELECT-CLIENTS]`

**What to Expect:** Direct access to the professional executing the work, no handoffs/filtering. Clear milestones, regular previews. Modern tools + disciplined process = faster delivery, transparent reporting.

**FAQs:** Most projects start within 1 week of approved scope · client involvement = direction/feedback at key milestones, heavy lifting handled by YDM · mid‑project scope changes discussed openly and documented, no hidden fees · no minimum project size, small projects get same structured process.
