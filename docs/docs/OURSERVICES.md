## Engagement Types — The Legend

Every service on the menu is sold as one or more of these four models:

- **Project** — Defined scope, fixed price or milestone billing, clear start and end date. Used for builds, audits, and one-time strategic work.
- **Retainer** — Ongoing monthly engagement with recurring deliverables. Typically carries a 3–6 month minimum commitment. Used for continuous management and optimization.
- **Sprint** — A short, intensive, time-boxed engagement (2–6 weeks) targeting one specific outcome. Used when a client needs a focused push without a full retainer commitment.
- **Bundle** — Multiple services combined into a single coordinated engagement with one timeline and one point of contact. Used for full-system launches and cross-pillar packages.

Each service below notes which models apply.
