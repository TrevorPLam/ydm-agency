## Master Inventory of All Marketing Website Pages (Complete)

### 1. Homepage
**Purpose:** Instantly convey who you are, what you do, who it’s for, and the primary next step.  
**Key Practices:** Single benefit-driven headline + subhead above fold, one primary CTA, social proof visible immediately, sub-2s load time, clear visual hierarchy.  
**Pillar Optimizations:**  
- **SEO/AEO:** Branded + top service keyword; Organization/LocalBusiness schema.  
- **Paid Ads:** Message match from ad to hero.  
- **Branding:** Distinct voice, color, logo lockup.  
- **Content:** Concise USP.  
- **Automation:** Dynamic hero by UTM source.  
- **Reputation:** Embedded review snippet.  
- **Analytics:** Scroll depth, CTA clicks, exit intent.

### 2. About / Team Page
**Purpose:** Build human trust and demonstrate qualification.  
**Key Practices:** Real photos, mission tied to outcomes, relevance over history, clear path to services.  
**Pillar Optimizations:**  
- **SEO/AEO:** Person schema on bios, founder/company name keywords; Organization schema with sameAs links.  
- **Branding:** Culture, voice.  
- **Automation:** Team data from single JSON/CMS source.  
- **Reputation:** Awards, certifications, press.  
- **Analytics:** Time on page vs. follow-through.

### 3. Careers / Job Listings Page
**Purpose:** Attract qualified talent and communicate employer brand.  
**Key Practices:** Open positions with descriptions, benefits/culture section, application form or ATS integration, team testimonials, clear application process.  
**Pillar Optimizations:**  
- **SEO/AEO:** "Jobs at [company]" keywords; Organization schema with hiringOrganization.  
- **Reputation:** Glassdoor/LinkedIn links, culture content.  
- **Automation:** ATS sync, auto-responder, candidate tracking.  
- **Analytics:** Application rate, source tracking.

### 4. Contact Page
**Purpose:** Convert ready prospects with minimal friction.  
**Key Practices:** Multi-channel (form, phone, email), minimal fields, privacy reassurance, map embed if applicable.  
**Pillar Optimizations:**  
- **SEO/AEO:** ContactPage schema, “company name contact” keywords.  
- **Automation:** CRM entry, auto-responder, internal alert.  
- **Reputation:** Response-time trust line.  
- **Analytics:** Form abandonment tracking.

### 5. Privacy Policy
**Purpose:** Comply with data protection regulations (GDPR/CCPA) and build trust through transparency.  
**Key Practices:** Plain language, clear data collection/use details, user rights (access, deletion, portability), links in global footer, last updated date.  
**Pillar Optimizations:**  
- **Automation:** One master legal text block across all monorepo sites; push updates globally.  
- **Reputation:** Transparent privacy practices reduce anxiety.  
- **Content:** Accessible, no legalese.  
- **SEO/AEO:** Usually noindex, but a trust signal.

### 6. Terms of Service / Terms & Conditions
**Purpose:** Define legal terms of use, liabilities, and account rules for site visitors.  
**Key Practices:** Clear usage rules, liability limitations, account termination policy, dispute resolution, links in global footer, last updated date.  
**Pillar Optimizations:**  
- **Automation:** One master legal text block across all monorepo sites; push updates globally.  
- **Reputation:** Clear terms protect both parties.  
- **Content:** Accessible, no legalese.  
- **SEO/AEO:** Usually noindex, but a trust signal.

### 7. Cookie Policy (Detailed Declaration)
**Purpose:** Disclose every cookie used, separate from the consent banner.  
**Key Practices:** Table of cookie name, provider, purpose, duration; categorized by necessary/analytics/marketing; linked from consent manager.  
**Pillar Optimizations:**  
- **Reputation:** Detailed disclosure = trust.  
- **Automation:** Generate dynamically from cookie scan tool.  
- **SEO/AEO:** Noindex.

### 8. Accessibility Statement
**Purpose:** Demonstrate commitment to inclusive design and meet legal requirements.  
**Key Practices:** Stated WCAG conformance level, known limitations, contact method for issues, last updated date.  
**Pillar Optimizations:**  
- **Reputation:** Signals inclusion.  
- **Automation:** Linked globally in footer template.  
- **Content:** Clear, actionable.

### 9. Cookie Settings / Consent Manager
**Purpose:** Granular consent control for tracking.  
**Key Practices:** Categorized toggles, easy to access from footer, consent log, re-prompt on changes.  
**Pillar Optimizations:**  
- **Analytics:** Consent rates impact data accuracy.  
- **Automation:** Dynamically block scripts based on user preferences.  
- **Reputation:** Respecting choice builds trust.  
- **SEO/AEO:** Not indexed.

### 10. HTML Sitemap
**Purpose:** Human-readable, linked index of all important pages for users and search engines.  
**Key Practices:** Hierarchical list organized by site section, clean styling, linked from footer, auto-updated.  
**Pillar Optimizations:**  
- **SEO/AEO:** Aids crawl and indexation.  
- **Automation:** Generate dynamically from monorepo route map.  
- **Analytics:** Low traffic, but used by bots.

### 11. Search Results Page
**Purpose:** Display relevant content for on-site search queries.  
**Key Practices:** Prominent query display, results with snippets, filters by content type, helpful “no results” state.  
**Pillar Optimizations:**  
- **Analytics:** Internal search data reveals content gaps.  
- **Automation:** AI-powered personalization.  
- **SEO/AEO:** Not indexed; informs keyword strategy.

### 12. 404 / Error Page
**Purpose:** Retain visitors who land on a missing URL and guide them to useful content.  
**Key Practices:** Helpful tone, search bar, links to homepage/main sections, never a dead end.  
**Pillar Optimizations:**  
- **Analytics:** Track 404 occurrences to identify broken links.  
- **Automation:** Log errors, suggest 301 redirects, alert team.  
- **Reputation:** Well-designed error page turns frustration into positive moment.  
- **SEO/AEO:** Proper 404 status code.

### 13. Coming Soon / Maintenance Page
**Purpose:** Collect interest pre-launch or inform visitors during downtime.  
**Key Practices:** Countdown or clear status message, email capture, social links, brand visuals.  
**Pillar Optimizations:**  
- **Analytics:** Track signups as pre-launch leads; monitor downtime traffic.  
- **Automation:** Add emails to launch sequence; auto-disable when maintenance ends.  
- **Paid Ads:** Use as waitlist destination for pre-launch campaigns.  
- **SEO/AEO:** Serve 503 during maintenance; noindex coming soon page.

### 14. Login / Register Page
**Purpose:** Secure access with minimal abandonment.  
**Key Practices:** Social login, single email field first, visible password recovery, account benefit statement.  
**Pillar Optimizations:**  
- **Analytics:** Attempt/failure rates.  
- **Automation:** Welcome email, abandoned registration recovery.  
- **SEO/AEO:** Typically noindex.

### 15. Password Reset Page
**Purpose:** Self-service password recovery.  
**Key Practices:** Simple email field, submit button, success state with “Check your email” message, brand-consistent.  
**Pillar Optimizations:**  
- **Analytics:** Reset request volumes signal login friction.  
- **Automation:** Trigger secure email with reset token.  
- **Reputation:** Smooth UX reduces frustration.  
- **SEO/AEO:** Typically noindex.

### 16. Email Preference Center
**Purpose:** Let subscribers manage email types and frequency.  
**Key Practices:** Checkbox per category, clear descriptions, instant sync confirmation, linked from all emails.  
**Pillar Optimizations:**  
- **Automation:** Real-time sync to ESP.  
- **Analytics:** Preference patterns inform segmentation.  
- **Reputation:** Reduces spam complaints.  
- **SEO/AEO:** Noindex; compliance asset.

### 17. Unsubscribe Confirmation Page
**Purpose:** Confirm successful unsubscription and offer alternatives.  
**Key Practices:** Confirmation message, link to preference center to re-subscribe selectively, brand tone.  
**Pillar Optimizations:**  
- **Automation:** Immediate list removal.  
- **Reputation:** Leaves a positive last impression.  
- **Analytics:** Track unsub reasons if given.

### 18. Blog / Resource Hub
**Purpose:** Attract, educate, nurture top-of-funnel via content.  
**Key Practices:** Topic clusters, search/filter, CTAs on every article, real bylines.  
**Pillar Optimizations:**

### 19. Blog Article / Post

**Pillar Optimizations:**

### 20. Case Studies
**Purpose:** Prove results with data-backed client stories.

**Pillar Optimizations:**

- **Analytics:** Downloads and contact path tracking.

### 21. Testimonials / Reviews Page
**Purpose:** Concentrated social proof to overcome skepticism.  
**Key Practices:** Real names/photos/roles, filterable by service, aggregate rating + schema, embedded third-party reviews.  
**Pillar Optimizations:**

### 22. FAQ / Help Center

**Pillar Optimizations:**

### 23. Service Overview Page
**Purpose:** Present all core services at a glance for quick navigation.

**Pillar Optimizations:**

### 24. Service/Product Detail Page (Pillar)
